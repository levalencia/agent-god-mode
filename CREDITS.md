## Tier 1 Lifecycle Skills

The 20 production-grade engineering skills located in the `skills/` directory are sourced from:

- **Repository:** [addyosmani/agent-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills)
- **Author:** Addy Osmani
- **License:** MIT License (reproduced below)

These skills were integrated into Agent God Mode to provide a **two-tier hybrid architecture** that combines software engineering lifecycle enforcement with massive domain-specific knowledge retrieval.
